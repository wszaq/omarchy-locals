**Identification:** `wszaq.locals` is the monitor and folder glyph immediately before `0/0`. The plain monitor after `0/0` is `omarchy.monitor`.

**Why it blends:** The two glyphs use a similar monitor shape, size, weight, and color. The nearby bar icons use the same visual style.

**Recommended glyph:** `L` (U+004C, Latin capital letter L). It reads as “locals” without another monitor shape.
